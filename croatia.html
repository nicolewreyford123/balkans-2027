Croatia

Candidate country · the deck runs Zagreb → Split → Dubrovnik · 4 places in the field notes

The coast that launched this whole plan: a Roman palace lived in as a town, walls walked at opening time, sixteen terraced lakes inland — and, from the Croatia deck, a capital worth two nights before any of it. Croatia joined Schengen and the euro in 2023, which makes it the easiest paperwork on the list. The deck's working figures: R32,500 including flights around R15,000 — planning numbers, not quotes.
