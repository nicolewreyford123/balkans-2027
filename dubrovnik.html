Dubrovnik

No. 02 · Croatia · Days 3–5 · 3 nights — walls, an island, and a slow day

Our first 3-night stop. This is where the freed Mostar night went — the walls, Lokrum, and a lazy day all genuinely fit now.

The list

No.ThingNotes
1City wallsThe signature walk. Go at opening time — seriously. ~2h, bring water. Midday in season is brutal and packed.
2Cable car to SrđThe mountain behind town — best panorama of the old city and the Adriatic. Walk down if knees allow.
3Swim at BužaThe cliff-side bars outside the walls; swim ladders straight into the sea.
4Lokrum island15-min ferry: botanical gardens, peacocks, a saltwater "Dead Sea" lake.
5Cavtat or Trsteno arboretumQuiet bus-trip options for a change of scene.
6A full lazy daySwim, eat, repeat. Endorsed by the itinerary.
7Bosnia add-on: TrebinjeHalf-day, 30–45 min away — our preferred Bosnia taste. Mostar itself gets its own page.

Eat & drink: walk the Stradun after the day-trippers leave — it's a different town at 8pm; seafood and local white wine (Pošip).Field note · Balkans 2027
