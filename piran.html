Piran & the coast

Candidate country · Slovenia · the Venetian teardrop on a 47-km coast

Slovenia's coast is 47 kilometres long, which sounds like a typo until you arrive. Piran is the postcard: a Venetian town on a teardrop peninsula, and the closing scene of both route decks.

The peninsula

Tartini Square opens the town, named for the violinist born on the spot; from there the lanes go salt-bleached and Venetian, and the town walls lead up to the cathedral bell tower with the whole Adriatic behind it. The deck prescribes a long seafood lunch and no schedule — good prescriptions, both. Sleep in budget-friendly Koper or nearby per the 10-day plan, and commute the short hop to Piran.

Swims & neighbours

Swimming here is off rocky and paved access points — bring water shoes, the deck's most repeated instruction — with Izola and Portorož a local bus away for easier beach afternoons. Slow days fill themselves: the Sečovlje salt pans for the salt-making heritage (check entry costs), or the Lipica stud farm, the original home of the Lipizzaners, since the deck will ask.

On the way: the caves & the cliff castle

The 7-day plan's detour breaks the bus ride south: Postojna Cave, one of Europe's great karst systems, toured partly by an underground train, about 50 minutes from Ljubljana; then Predjama Castle, fifteen minutes on, built into the mouth of a cliff face like a bird deciding on architecture. Book the castle transfer in advance — the deck is firm on that point.

Where the decks put it: the 10-day plan spends nights 7–9 on the Koper/Piran coast and drives (buses) home on day 10 from here; the 7-day plan arrives for a single night — via the Postojna detour above — before its last hop to Kranjska Gora. Either way this is the closing scene. The 2h 30m bus from Ljubljana is the longest leg in either plan; from here, Croatia's Istrian coast is the natural next move if the Alps-then-coast combination wins.

Eat & drink: seafood in Piran, budget pizza or an apartment dinner on ordinary nights, gelato on the waterfront at sunset. Currency is the euro.Field note · Balkans 2027
