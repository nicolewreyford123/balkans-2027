Montenegro

Candidate country · the bay, the beaches and the mountains · 5 places in the field notes

A country that does three different holidays at once: the fjord-like Bay of Kotor with Perast and the Ladder of Kotor, the Budva Riviera's beaches with Sveti Stefan's postcard causeway, and the national-park mountains — Lovćen above the bay, Durmitor's canyons and Black Lake up north. Everything is euro-denominated (unilaterally — Montenegro isn't in the eurozone, which nobody notices) and nothing is more than a few hours from anything else. No deck figures here — Montenegro comes from the original research and Nicole's map list.
