Krujë & Bovilla

No. 12 · Albania · Day trip from Tirana · reservoir and mountain

An easy half-day out of Tirana: the Bovilla reservoir dam with Gamti mountain hanging over it, plus a rock-climbing spot on the map list for anyone with the energy.

The list

No.ThingNotes
1Dam walkTwenty minutes across the top of the dam — the classic photo with Gamti behind. Go early: parking gets tight by 10am.
2Hike to Gamti4–5h return for fit legs; the trail leaves from the far end of the dam. Bring water — no shade.
3Climbing sessionThe pinned climbing lines suit an afternoon top-rope trip if someone brings gear.

Eat & drink: back in Krujë proper — the old bazaar's cafés and qofte. All lek cash at the reservoir.Field note · Balkans 2027
