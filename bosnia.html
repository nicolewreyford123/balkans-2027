Bosnia & Herzegovina

Chosen country · from its own route deck · 3 places in the field notes

The deck's country: an 11-night run Sarajevo → Konjic & Mostar → Jajce — Baščaršija and the Tunnel of Hope, the Stari Most and Blagaj, the Pliva waterfall in the middle of Jajce — with the deck's own extension south into Kotor Bay, Perast and Lovćen. Every route now gives Bosnia nights — Mostar on all three, with Sarajevo added on routes 2 and 3. The deck's budget slide carries no numbers yet. Currency is the convertible mark (KM); Bosnia is outside Schengen, but the Schengen visa from the Croatia application gets us in visa-free.
