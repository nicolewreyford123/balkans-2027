Komani Lake & Lumi i Shalës

No. 11 · Albania · The fjord-like ferry day

The slow boat through Albania's biggest gorge — limestone walls, side valleys and a river at the far end that feels like a different country. Two pinned spots cover the whole route.

The list

No.ThingNotes
1The ferry crossing~3h one way through the gorge. Day-return is doable; the ferry runs on a schedule, so confirm the day before.
2Shala river timeSwim, rent a kayak, lunch on the platforms — most boats give you a couple of hours at the far end.
3Park before the tunnelNicole's tip: the drop-off at the dock is tight — park before the tunnel and walk down.
4Alternate: Valbona through-tripSome boats connect onward to Valbona valley instead of returning — only if the group wants the full mountain crossing.

Eat & drink: grilled trout by the Shala river; bring lek in cash for the boat tickets and river-platform lunches.Field note · Balkans 2027
