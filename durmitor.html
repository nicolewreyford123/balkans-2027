Durmitor & Žabljak

No. 08 · Montenegro · Northern mountains · lakes, rivers and canyons

Montenegro's other half — limestone peaks, glacial lakes and the Tara canyon, the deepest in Europe. Six pinned spots cluster here, and they're all reachable without organised tours once you're in Žabljak.

The list

No.ThingNotes
1Black Lake circuitEasy 45-min walk around the water — or longer if you head into the forest. The no-effort Durmitor day.
2Tara raftingHalf-day on the rapids through the canyon — book the morning run, water is released for it.
3Mrtvica canyon walkFollow the river up the gorge past "the gates" — bring shoes you don't mind getting wet.
4Sedlo pass viewpointsDrive or hitch the old road through the park for high plateau views.

Eat & drink: mountain portions — lamb under sač, kačamak, kajmak. Currency is the euro; cash for village konobas.Field note · Balkans 2027
