Mostar

No. 04 · Bosnia & Herzegovina · Day trip, or a 4-night base in its own route deck

A bridge town with real Ottoman bones. Watch for jumpers collecting donations before they leap off Stari Most — the bridge gets its drama from them too. The new Bosnia deck gives Mostar four nights as the base for Herzegovina, which upgrades it from day trip to proper stop in the orders where Bosnia comes first.
