Balkans 2027

The trip is decided. The plan: pick two or three of the five countries and combine them into one forward route — the decision is which.

The five candidates

  • Croatia — Diocletian's Split, Dubrovnik's walls, Plitvice's lakes. Split, Dubrovnik, Plitvice.
  • Bosnia & Herzegovina — Mostar's Stari Most and the Blagaj tekija, on the standing route as day trip or overnight.
  • Montenegro — the bay of Kotor, the Budva beaches, Lovćen and Durmitor. Kotor & the Bay, Budva Riviera, Lovćen, Durmitor.
  • Albania — Shkodër, the Komani ferry, Tirana, Berat's thousand windows and the southern riviera. Shkodër, Tirana, Berat.
  • Slovenia — the new candidate, from two route decks: Ljubljana, Bled & Bohinj, Piran & the coast.
  • Flights & visas — the paperwork that shapes the decision: one Schengen visa, country-by-country passport rules, and flight routings for every option.
  • The itinerary — the combinations on the table, and one of them already worked out end to end: the southern three, day by day, with the clickable map.
