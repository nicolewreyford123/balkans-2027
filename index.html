Balkans 2027

Three countries chosen. Flights have narrowed it to two routes, Zagreb in and Podgorica out; now we pick the order.

The three countries

  • Croatia — Diocletian's Split, Dubrovnik's walls, Plitvice's lakes, and the deck's Zagreb opener. Zagreb, Split, Dubrovnik, Plitvice.
  • Bosnia & Herzegovina — Sarajevo, Mostar and Jajce from the Bosnia deck, or day trips from Dubrovnik on a coastal route. Sarajevo, Mostar, Jajce.
  • Montenegro — the bay of Kotor, the Budva beaches, Lovćen and Durmitor. Kotor & the Bay, Budva Riviera, Lovćen, Durmitor.
  • Flights & visas — the paperwork and the gateways that will shape the route: one Schengen visa, country-by-country passport rules, and working fares into Zagreb, Sarajevo, Split, Dubrovnik, Tivat and Podgorica.
  • The two routes — both start in Zagreb and finish in Podgorica, with all three countries in each; one goes coast-first, the other Bosnia-first. No route chosen yet; the map numbers every main city and site across the three. Each country also has its own page holding all its field-notes places.
