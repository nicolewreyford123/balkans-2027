Balkans 2027

Three countries chosen. The dates are fixed at 27 May – 12 June 2027; flights have narrowed it to three routes, Zagreb in and Podgorica out. Now we pick the order.

The three countries

  • Croatia — Diocletian's Split, Dubrovnik's walls, Plitvice's lakes, and the deck's Zagreb opener. Zagreb, Split, Dubrovnik, Plitvice.
  • Bosnia & Herzegovina — Sarajevo, Mostar and Jajce from the Bosnia deck; every route sleeps in Bosnia. Sarajevo, Mostar, Jajce.
  • Montenegro — the bay of Kotor, the Budva beaches, Lovćen and Durmitor. Kotor & the Bay, Budva Riviera, Lovćen, Durmitor.
  • Flights & visas — the paperwork and the gateways that will shape the route: one Schengen visa, country-by-country passport rules, and working fares into Zagreb, Sarajevo, Split, Dubrovnik, Tivat and Podgorica.
  • The three routes — all start in Zagreb on 27 May, leave after four fixed nights on the morning of 31 May, and finish in Podgorica for the 12 June flight. One coast-first, one Bosnia-first, and one that folds Sarajevo in before the bay. Each has its own page with a workable split of the twelve route nights and the trade-offs.
