The decision

The trip is decided; the country line-up is not. The idea, from the start: choose two or three of the five countries and combine them into one forward-moving route. Five are in play — Croatia, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Montenegro, Albania and Slovenia — and every pairing that chains together geographically is a fair candidate. Nothing is decided and no combination is ruled out; see the Flights & visas page for the paperwork behind each choice. The southern three are already worked out end to end below, so there's a concrete plan to argue with.

The combinations on the table

How the trip gets built: pick two or three countries that chain together, fly into one end and out of the other, and move in one direction only. These four are the live options — none is chosen.

  • The southern three — Croatia + Montenegro + Albania — Split → Dubrovnik → Kotor → Shkodër → Tirana → Berat, with Bosnia visited on day trips from Dubrovnik. The only combination worked out day by day so far — the full plan is below. Open-jaw: in Split (SPU), out Tirana (TIA).
  • Bosnia first, Montenegro after — Bosnia & Herzegovina + Montenegro — from the new Bosnia deck: Sarajevo (4 nights: Baščaršija, the Tunnel of Hope, Vrelo Bosne) → Konjic and Mostar (4: Stari Most, Blagaj, Počitelj, Kravica falls, rafting) → Jajce (2: Pliva waterfall, medieval fortress) → back to Sarajevo (1), with the deck's own extension south into Kotor Bay, Perast and Lovćen. Flights via Sarajevo, or in Sarajevo and out of Dubrovnik.
  • Alps then coast — Slovenia + Croatia — Ljubljana → Bled & Bohinj → Piran, then down the Adriatic side to Split or Dubrovnik for the coastal finish. In Ljubljana (LJU), out Split or Dubrovnik. The two route decks plus the southern plan give both halves.
  • Bosnia paired with the coast — Bosnia & Herzegovina + Croatia — Sarajevo and Mostar joined straight onto the Dalmatian coast: Mostar → Dubrovnik → Split, in Sarajevo and out Split or Dubrovnik. The shortest, simplest two-country version.
  • Albania alone — the girls-trip deck — the deck plans all ten nights in one country: Tirana → Berat → Llogara + Dhërmi → Himarë + Gjipe → Sarandë, Ksamil + Butrint → Blue Eye + Gjirokastër → back to Tirana, accommodation around 3–4 bases. The deck's planning figures: R11,200–R18,580 per person excluding flights — working numbers, not quotes.

The rule that shapes all of them: countries chain along shared borders into one direction, the flights go open-jaw at the two ends, and nights are spent rather than moved. Whatever wins, the day-by-day below gets rebuilt around the same principles.

Why this combination

Four reasons the southern three won the first full write-up — the same tests any other combination has to pass.

1One direction onlyEvery move is forward along the coast — no backtracking, no wasted travel days.
2The right windowLate May / early June: the sea is swimmable-ish, towns aren't crushed yet, mountain roads are clear.
3Three countries, not fourFewer currencies, fewer borders, and one less 3-hour bus leg than the four-country version.

Day by day

The full 14-day plan for the southern three — Croatia, Montenegro and Albania, with Bosnia as day trips. If a different combination wins, this gets rebuilt around it on the same principles: one direction, nights spent not moved.

Days 1–2 · Split, Croatia — 2 nights

Arrive & explore the palace

  • D1 — Arrive SPU. Evening in Diocletian's Palace, drink on the Riva.
  • D2 — Palace + cathedral, climb Marjan hill for the sunset, swim at Bačvice. Alternative: day tour to Krka waterfalls, or Salona + Trogir by local bus.

Sleep: inside or just outside the palace walls.

Day 3 · Split → Dubrovnik — 3 nights

Coastal transfer (~4.5h)

  • Bus along the coast (sit left, sea side) — or the catamaran if it's running: similar time, far nicer.
  • Arrive with the afternoon intact. First evening: walk the Stradun once the day-trippers leave.

Sleep: inside the walls is magical and noisy; Ploče or Lapad is calmer.

Days 4–5 · Dubrovnik

The comfortable stop

  • D4 — City walls at opening time (go early, seriously). Cable car to Srđ. Swim at Buža.
  • D5 — Lokrum island, or bus to Cavtat / Trsteno arboretum. Or a full lazy day: swim, eat, repeat.
  • The walls, an island, and a slow day all fit now — no rushing.
Days 6–8 · Dubrovnik → Kotor, Montenegro — 3 nights

The fjord-like bay

  • D6 — Bus ~2.5–3h with the border crossing. Evening in the old town.
  • D7 — Kotor old town, then the Ladder of Kotor: bus/taxi to Krstac and walk back down. Best free mountain day of the trip.
  • D8 — Morning: Perast + boat to Our Lady of the Rocks. Afternoon: Lovćen serpentine + Njeguši village, pršut and cheese tasting. (Or split these across D7–D8 if the pace feels tight.)

Sleep: inside Kotor's walls, or Dobrota across the water for quiet.

Day 9 · Kotor → Shkodër, Albania — 1 night

The long leg (~4–5h)

  • Usually with a change at Podgorica, crossing at Hani i Hotit. Some years a direct seasonal bus runs.
  • Evening: Rozafa castle above the city, walk the pedestrian street.

Sleep: Shkodër centre.

Days 10–11 · Shkodër → Tirana — 2 nights

Lake day + capital

  • D10 — Morning: Lake Skadar boat trip from Virpazar, or a Koman Lake ferry day tour (long day — only if you're happy arriving in Tirana late). Bus to Tirana ~2h, frequent.
  • D11 — Tirana: Bunk'Art 1 or 2, Dajti Express cable car, Blloku for dinner.
Days 12–13 · Tirana → Berat — 2 nights

The city of a thousand windows

  • D12 — Bus ~2.5h from the South/West terminal; arrive by early afternoon. Mangalem & Gorica quarters.
  • D13 — The castle, Onufri Museum. Optional: Osumi canyon walk, or a wine tasting at one of the Cobo/Çobo estates.

Sleep: a restored Ottoman house in Mangalem.

Day 14 · Berat → Tirana airport

Home

  • Bus back to Tirana, then airport bus or taxi. Allow 4h buffer before an international flight.

Border note. The coastal road east of Dubrovnik clips through Bosnia at Neum (~9km). Since the Pelješac Bridge opened, many buses stay in Croatia the whole way — either way is painless. Just carry your passport in your day bag, not the luggage hold, and don't panic at two exit/entry stamps.

Bosnia: day trips, or a trip of its own

Bosnia now has its own route deck, so it competes two ways — as day trips bolted onto the southern combination, or as the backbone of a Bosnia-first combination (see the combinations above). The day-trip options, ranked by how well they fit a relaxed trip:

Best fit

1 · Trebinje — half-day from Dubrovnik

30–45 min over the border. Ottoman-era old town, plane trees, a famous wine region, almost no coach traffic. Doable as a late-afternoon/evening trip so our Dubrovnik day stays intact.

If we only do one Bosnia thing, do this.

The classic

2 · Mostar — full day from Dubrovnik

Organised day tours run ~10–11h with the Stari Most, bazaar, often Počitelj or Blagaj. The catch: it eats one of our two full Dubrovnik days — walls, Lokrum and Mostar don't all fit in 3 nights.

Nearly free

3 · Neum — transit stop

Bosnia's 20km of coast, right on the Dubrovnik→Kotor road. Cheaper food and drink, a small beach. Useful as a swim-and-lunch break on the Kotor transfer.

Don't try to "do Mostar on the way" between Split and Dubrovnik in one day — you'd arrive with luggage at 7pm having seen the bridge for three rushed hours. If Mostar matters to the group, it gets a night; otherwise use option 1 or 3.

And if Bosnia leads instead: the deck's 11-night plan runs Sarajevo (4 nights) → Konjic & Mostar (4) → Jajce (2) → Sarajevo (1) — Baščaršija and the Latin Bridge, the Tunnel of Hope and War Childhood museums, Vrelo Bosne's springs, the Stari Most and Kujundžiluk bazaar, Blagaj, Počitelj and the Kravica waterfalls, rafting on the Neretva, and the Fortica skywalk above Mostar — with the deck's own suggestion to extend south into Montenegro (Kotor Bay, Perast, Lovćen). The deck's budget slide carries no numbers yet; those get filled in before this combination can be compared properly.

Options if we want to tweak

  • More mountains? Add a night in Theth or Valbona via an organised tour from Shkodër — pushes the pace toward "moderate".
  • An island? A night on Hvar or Korčula, Split → island → Dubrovnik by catamaran. Lovely, but adds cost and seasonal-transport risk.
  • Only 7–10 days? Dubrovnik (3) → Kotor (3) → Tirana (2), fly out of TIA. Drop Split entirely — the only relaxed version in a week.
  • Bosnia as a proper stop? Mostar slots in between Split and Dubrovnik as a single night — one ~3h bus each day. Or promote Bosnia to a full combination of its own — the deck plan above.

Book early & get ready

Book early — late May is already busy:

  • Dubrovnik & Kotor accommodation — cruise traffic peaks, prices climb fast after mid-May.
  • Koman Lake / Skadar tours — they sell out in June.
  • Fixed-date items: our two flights.

Money & paperwork:

  • Currencies: Croatia = euro · Montenegro = euro (unilateral) · Albania = lek. Cards fine in Croatia & Montenegro; carry cash in Albania.
  • Schengen: Croatia counts against your 90/180 days; Montenegro and Albania don't.
  • Passport: most nationalities need 3+ months validity beyond departure — check yours, this is the #1 trip-killer.
  • Insurance: an EHIC/GHIC won't cover Montenegro or Albania — get real travel insurance.
